# Model cards: forecasting models of the tutorial

Checked on 2026-11-13 against the papers, the Hugging Face model cards, the READMEs, and the installed code (env `ml-time-series`: chronos-forecasting 2.3.2, timesfm 3.0.2, tirex-ts 1.4.2, tirex-2 0.5.0, timestral 1.0.0, tfc-t0 0.6.1, tabicl 2.2.0).
"Code" marks a value read in the installed package or the checkpoint config.
Benchmark claims are author-reported unless stated otherwise.

Points to keep in mind for the notebook:

- **Truncation without a warning.** Chronos-2, TiRex and TiRex-2 keep only their last 8192, 2048 and 2048 steps. A longer context changes nothing for them.
- **TiRex-2 forecasts at most 320 steps** in the released code and truncates longer horizons with a warning. A 2-week hourly horizon (336) does not fit.
- **No paper yet** for TimesFM 3 and Timestral. t0-gamma is newer than the t0 paper (which covers alpha and beta).
- **Leaderboard claims overlap.** TimesFM 3, Timestral, TiRex-2 and TimeH2O each claim first place on GIFT-Eval or fev-bench, depending on the date and the metric.

## Chronos (T5)

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: Amazon. Paper and code released 13 Mar 2024. Apache-2.0. Five checkpoints `amazon/chronos-t5-{tiny,mini,small,base,large}` with 8M, 20M, 46M, 200M and 710M parameters. The tutorial uses `chronos-t5-small` (46M).
- **Architecture type**: Encoder-decoder, the T5 language model unchanged except for a 4096-token vocabulary. Univariate only. No covariates and no mixing across series.
- **Tokenization / patching**: No patching, one token per time step. Mean scaling (by the mean absolute value of the context), then uniform quantization into 4094 bins on [-15, 15], plus PAD and EOS tokens (code: `MeanScaleUniformBins`, `n_tokens=4096`).
- **Context**: 512 steps (code: checkpoint `context_length=512`). A longer context is truncated to its last 512 values.
- **Horizon**: Autoregressive, one token per step, sampled like a language model. Trained with a prediction length of 64 (code: `prediction_length=64`). Beyond 64 the pipeline warns, appends the sampled paths to the context and samples the next 64-step chunk.
- **Output**: Sample paths (default 20, code: `num_samples=20`, temperature 1, top_k 50). Quantiles are empirical quantiles of the samples, at any level asked.
- **API**: `chronos-forecasting==2.3.2`. Tutorial wrapper `Chronos`: `from chronos import BaseChronosPipeline`, `pipe = BaseChronosPipeline.from_pretrained("amazon/chronos-t5-small", device_map=DEVICE)`, `q, mean = pipe.predict_quantiles(torch.tensor(ctx), prediction_length=h, quantile_levels=QUANTILES)`. Input `(n_series, T)`, output `q` of shape `(n_series, h, 9)`. The wrapper calls it in chunks of 4 series to bound sampling memory.
- **Training**: 28 public datasets (about 890K univariate series, about 84B observations), plus 10M TSMixup augmentations and 1M KernelSynth synthetic series (Gaussian processes). 200K training steps. No fine-tuning in the release.
- **Benchmarks reported**: Chronos Benchmark I (15 in-domain datasets) and Benchmark II (27 zero-shot datasets), 42 datasets in total, with WQL and MASE. Author-reported: zero-shot accuracy comparable to and sometimes better than models trained on each dataset.
- **Paper / links**: arXiv 2403.07815 ("Chronos: Learning the Language of Time Series"). https://huggingface.co/amazon/chronos-t5-small . https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting

## Chronos-Bolt

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: Amazon. Released 26 Nov 2024. Apache-2.0. Four checkpoints `amazon/chronos-bolt-{tiny,mini,small,base}` with 9M, 21M, 48M and 205M parameters. The tutorial uses `chronos-bolt-small` (48M).
- **Architecture type**: Encoder-decoder (T5-efficient backbone). Univariate only. No covariates and no mixing across series.
- **Tokenization / patching**: No quantization. Non-overlapping input patches of 16 steps, embedded together with their missing-value mask (code: `input_patch_size=16`, embedding input dim `2 * 16`). Per-series standardization (mean and std of the context, code: `InstanceNorm`, no arcsinh).
- **Context**: 2048 steps (code: checkpoint `context_length=2048`). A longer context is truncated to its last 2048 values.
- **Horizon**: Direct multi-step. The decoder outputs the quantiles of the next 64 steps at once (code: `prediction_length=64`). Beyond 64 the pipeline warns, appends each of the 9 predicted quantile paths to the context, predicts the next 64 steps, and reduces the 81 resulting paths back to 9 quantiles. It repeats until the horizon is covered.
- **Output**: Quantiles 0.1, 0.2, ..., 0.9 (code: checkpoint `quantiles`). Other levels are interpolated with a warning.
- **API**: `chronos-forecasting==2.3.2`. Tutorial wrapper `ChronosBolt` reuses `Chronos`: same `BaseChronosPipeline.from_pretrained("amazon/chronos-bolt-small", ...)` and `predict_quantiles(torch.tensor(ctx), prediction_length=h, quantile_levels=QUANTILES)`. Input `(n_series, T)`, output `(n_series, h, 9)`.
- **Training**: "Nearly 100 billion time series observations" (model card). The corpus composition and any synthetic data are not stated for Bolt (checked the HF card and the GitHub README).
- **Benchmarks reported**: 27 datasets (Chronos Benchmark II) with WQL and MASE. Author-reported: 5% lower error, up to 250 times faster and 20 times more memory-efficient than the original Chronos of the same size. Bolt (Base) more accurate than Chronos (Large) and over 600 times faster.
- **Paper / links**: No dedicated paper (refers to arXiv 2403.07815). https://huggingface.co/amazon/chronos-bolt-small . https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting

## Chronos-2

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: Amazon. Released 20 Oct 2025 (report on arXiv 17 Oct 2025). Apache-2.0. `amazon/chronos-2` has 120M parameters. A 28M `autogluon/chronos-2-small` also exists.
- **Architecture type**: Encoder-only, following the T5 encoder (12 layers, d_model 768, code). Layers alternate time attention (within a series, RoPE) and group attention (across the series of a group at each patch). A group holds the target variates and their covariates, or several series with `cross_learning=True`. Takes past-only and known-future covariates, real or categorical.
- **Tokenization / patching**: Non-overlapping patches of 16 steps in and 16 steps out (code: `input_patch_size=16`, `output_patch_size=16`), with time index and mask as meta features. Standardization then arcsinh (code: `use_arcsinh=true`). A REG token separates context and future patches.
- **Context**: 8192 steps (code: checkpoint `context_length=8192`). Pretrained at 2048 then extended to 8192. A longer context is truncated to the last 8192 values. Asking for more resets it to 8192 with a warning.
- **Horizon**: Direct multi-step. Future patches are placeholder tokens filled in one forward pass. At most 64 output patches, so 1024 steps per pass (code: `max_output_patches=64`). Beyond 1024 the pipeline warns and unrolls autoregressively, appending 9 predicted quantile paths to the context.
- **Output**: 21 quantiles 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, ..., 0.95, 0.99 (code: checkpoint `quantiles`). Requested levels are selected from these, or interpolated when not in the list.
- **API**: `chronos-forecasting==2.3.2`. Tutorial wrapper `Chronos2`: `from chronos import Chronos2Pipeline`, `pipe = Chronos2Pipeline.from_pretrained("amazon/chronos-2", device_map=DEVICE)`, `q, mean = pipe.predict_quantiles(inputs, prediction_length=h, quantile_levels=QUANTILES, cross_learning=False)`. `inputs` is a list of dicts `{"target", "past_covariates", "future_covariates"}`. Output is a list with one tensor `(n_variates, h, 9)` per series. The wrapper stacks and squeezes to `(n_series, h, 9)`.
- **Training**: Real univariate data from the Chronos corpus and GIFT-Eval Pretrain (GIFT-Eval test splits excluded). Synthetic univariate data from TSI, TCM, AR, ETS and KernelSynth. All multivariate data is synthetic, built by "multivariatizers". Total size in time points is not stated (checked the arXiv HTML and HF card).
- **Benchmarks reported**: fev-bench (100 tasks), GIFT-Eval (97 tasks), Chronos Benchmark II (27 tasks). Author-reported: on fev-bench, 90.7% win rate and 47.3% skill score versus 80.8% and 42.6% for TiRex. State of the art among public models on all three. About 300 series per second on one A10G GPU.
- **Paper / links**: arXiv 2510.15821 ("Chronos-2: From Univariate to Universal Forecasting"). https://huggingface.co/amazon/chronos-2 . https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting

## TimesFM 2.5

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: Google Research. Released 15 Sep 2025. Apache-2.0 for code and weights. `google/timesfm-2.5-200m-pytorch` has 200M parameters, plus an optional 30M continuous quantile head (README).
- **Architecture type**: Decoder-only transformer (20 layers, d_model 1280, 16 heads, RoPE, code). Univariate. No cross-series mixing in the model. Covariates only through the external XReg linear regression (`forecast_with_covariates`), not used in the tutorial.
- **Tokenization / patching**: Input patches of 32 steps with their mask (code: `input_patch_len=32`, tokenizer input dim 64). Output patches of 128 steps (code: `output_patch_len=128`). Per-patch reversible normalization with running mean and std (code: `revin`, `update_running_stats`). No frequency indicator.
- **Context**: Up to 16384 steps (README "16k"). Code caps `max_context + max_horizon` at 16384 (`context_limit`). `max_context` is fixed at `compile()`. Longer inputs are truncated to the last `max_context` values, shorter ones are left-padded and masked. The tutorial compiles with `max_context=2048`.
- **Horizon**: Autoregressive over output patches. Each decoding step outputs 128 steps, whose median is fed back as new input patches (code: `num_decode_steps = (horizon - 1) // 128`). The horizon must not exceed `max_horizon`, set at compile time. With the continuous quantile head, `max_horizon` is capped at 1024 (code: `output_quantile_len=1024`). The tutorial compiles with `max_horizon=1024`.
- **Output**: Mean then quantiles 0.1, 0.2, ..., 0.9, shape `(n_series, h, 10)` (code and HF card).
- **API**: `timesfm==3.0.2` (`pip install timesfm[torch]`). Tutorial wrapper `TimesFM`: `import timesfm`, `m = timesfm.TimesFM_2p5_200M_torch.from_pretrained("google/timesfm-2.5-200m-pytorch")`, `m.compile(timesfm.ForecastConfig(max_context=2048, max_horizon=1024, normalize_inputs=True, use_continuous_quantile_head=True, fix_quantile_crossing=True))`, `point, q = m.forecast(horizon=h, inputs=list(ctx))`. Output `point` `(n_series, h)` and `q` `(n_series, h, 10)`. The wrapper drops index 0 (the mean) to get `(n_series, h, 9)`.
- **Training**: GIFT-Eval Pretrain, Wikimedia pageviews (to Nov 2023), Google Trends top queries (to end of 2022), and synthetic and augmented data (HF card). Total size in time points is not stated for 2.5 (checked HF card and GitHub README). No fine-tuning in the release, a LoRA fine-tuning example is in the repo.
- **Benchmarks reported**: GIFT-Eval. Author-reported (Google, Sep 2025, relayed by press, not seen on a primary page): first among zero-shot foundation models on GIFT-Eval for point (MASE) and probabilistic (CRPS) accuracy. The HF card and README report no numbers.
- **Paper / links**: No 2.5-specific paper. The card cites arXiv 2310.10688 ("A decoder-only foundation model for time-series forecasting", ICML 2024). https://huggingface.co/google/timesfm-2.5-200m-pytorch . https://github.com/google-research/timesfm

## TimesFM 3

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: Google Research. Released Aug 2026 (blog post dated 31 Aug 2026). Code Apache-2.0, weights under `timesfm-non-commercial-license-v1.0` (non-commercial, non-production use only). `google/timesfm-3.0-pytorch` has 330M parameters (blog, HF lists 0.3B).
- **Architecture type**: Decoder-only transformer (20 layers, d_model 1280, 16 heads, code). Layers alternate strictly causal attention over time within a series and full attention across variates at each time step. The variates are the targets, past-only covariates and past-and-future covariates. At most 32 variates per forward pass (code: `max_variates=32`). The `TimesFM3Evaluator` chunks larger inputs.
- **Tokenization / patching**: Input patches of 32 steps, output patches of 64 steps (code: `input_patch_len=32`, `output_patch_len=64`). Past-and-future covariate tokens also see their future patches. Per-series normalization with iterative RevIN refinement and optional linear detrending (code: `use_iterative_cpm_revin`, `use_linear_detrending`).
- **Context**: 15360 steps (code: `_MAX_CONTEXT_LENGTH = 15360`, `global_context`). Each batch uses its longest context rounded up to a multiple of 32, capped at 15360. Longer contexts are truncated to the most recent values (README, MLX section).
- **Horizon**: Non-autoregressive. Contiguous Patch Masking puts masked placeholder tokens over the whole horizon and predicts it in one forward pass (code: `decode` docstring, blog). The horizon is rounded up to a multiple of 64, and overlapping output patches are stitched (code: `use_stitching=True`). No maximum horizon is stated in the code, the README or the blog.
- **Output**: Median point forecast plus quantiles 0.1, 0.2, ..., 0.9, shape `(h, 9)` per target series (code: `ForecastOutput.quantiles`).
- **API**: `timesfm==3.0.2`, module `timesfm3`. Tutorial wrapper `TimesFM3`: `from timesfm3 import TimesFM3Forecaster`, `m = TimesFM3Forecaster.from_pretrained("google/timesfm-3.0-pytorch", device=DEVICE)`, `out = m.predict_batch(contexts=list(ctx), horizon=h, return_quantiles=True, make_positive=True, past_only_covariates=..., past_future_covariates=...)`. Each element of `out` has `.forecast` `(h,)` and `.quantiles` `(h, 9)` for a univariate context. Past-and-future covariates span `T + h` steps. The wrapper stacks to `(n_series, h, 9)`.
- **Training**: More than 1 trillion time points, real and synthetic (blog). Real data: GIFT-Eval Pretrain minus datasets overlapping fev-bench, Wikipedia pageviews (to Nov 2023), Google Trends top queries (to end of 2022). Synthetic and augmented data, generators not described (HF card and blog checked).
- **Benchmarks reported**: GIFT-Eval, fev-bench, TIME. Author-reported: rank 1 overall on fev-bench (100 tasks) and TIME (50 datasets, 98 tasks), rank 1 among foundation models on GIFT-Eval, for point and probabilistic metrics, against Chronos-2, Toto 2.0 and TimesFM 2.5.
- **Paper / links**: No TimesFM 3 paper found on arXiv (searched arXiv, the blog, the README and the HF card). Related: arXiv 2505.23719 (Contiguous Patch Masking) and arXiv 2310.10688 (TimesFM). Blog https://research.google/blog/timesfm-3-a-zero-shot-foundation-model-for-multivariate-forecasting/ . https://huggingface.co/google/timesfm-3.0-pytorch . https://github.com/google-research/timesfm

## TiRex

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: NXAI GmbH (Linz, Austria). arXiv v1 on 29 May 2025, NeurIPS 2025. Weights and code under the NXAI Community License (based on the Llama 3 license, with extra commercial terms for large companies). 35M parameters (HF card and paper).
- **Architecture type**: Recurrent xLSTM. 12 sLSTM blocks, embedding 512, 4 heads (paper, checkpoint hyperparameters). Univariate only: each series is forecast independently, no covariates.
- **Tokenization / patching**: Input and output patches of 32 steps. Per-series standard scaling (mean and std over the context, code `StandardScaler`). Each patch is embedded together with its missing-value mask, NaN filled with 0.
- **Context**: Trained with 2048 steps (paper, `train_ctx_len=2048` in the checkpoint). The code keeps the last 2048 steps and left-pads shorter contexts with NaN.
- **Horizon**: Produced patch by patch through the recurrent state. Future patches are fed as masked (NaN) inputs, not as the model's own predictions (paper: Contiguous Patch Masking). No maximum horizon in the code. Default is one new patch per forward pass (`max_accelerated_rollout_steps=1`). Optional frequency resampling for horizons of 100 steps or more (`resample_strategy="frequency"`, off by default).
- **Output**: 9 quantiles, 0.1 to 0.9, plus the median returned as "mean".
- **API**: `pip install tirex-ts` (1.4.2). Tutorial wrapper `TiRex`: `from tirex import load_model`, `model = load_model("NX-AI/TiRex", device=DEVICE)`, `quantiles, _ = model.forecast(context=torch.tensor(context), prediction_length=horizon, output_type="numpy")`. Input `(n_series, T)`, output `(n_series, horizon, 9)`.
- **Training**: 47.5M series in total. About 30M Chronos training series with TSMixup, about 15M synthetic Gaussian-process series (KernelSynth-style, length 4096), about 2.5M from the GIFT-Eval pretraining split. Augmentations: amplitude modulation, censoring, spike injection. 500k steps, batch 256 (paper). No post-training stated.
- **Benchmarks reported**: Author-reported. GIFT-Eval zero-shot CRPS 0.411 and MASE 0.647, claimed state of the art at publication. Chronos Benchmark II (Chronos-ZS) WQL 0.592 and MASE 0.776, best WQL, second-best MASE behind TabPFN-TS.
- **Paper / links**: arXiv 2505.23719. Model card https://huggingface.co/NX-AI/TiRex. Repo https://github.com/NX-AI/tirex. Docs https://nx-ai.github.io/tirex/.

## TiRex-2

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: NXAI GmbH. arXiv 2607.01204 submitted 1 July 2026 (the June 2026 date in the brief was not found in the sources). NeurIPS 2026 oral (README). Apache 2.0. 38.4M active parameters in univariate mode plus 44.1M for multivariate (82.5M total on HF).
- **Architecture type**: Recurrent xLSTM. 12 blocks alternating mLSTM and sLSTM, embedding 512 (config). Each block has a time mixer and a variate mixer. Targets and past covariates use a forward-only xLSTM. Known-future covariates also get a weight-tied reverse pass. The variate mixer is grouped multi-head attention with an asymmetric mask: covariates never read the targets.
- **Tokenization / patching**: Input and output patches of 32 steps, stride 32 (config). Reversible instance normalization, then arcsinh, with a bypass for binary {0,1} covariates (paper, `scaler_cfg`). Differencing test-time augmentation on by default (`tta_diff: true`).
- **Context**: Released config: `context_len` 2048. The code keeps the last 2048 + 320 steps of the padded window and left-pads shorter inputs with NaN. The paper describes a long-context post-training with 8192 steps of context, but the released config and code use 2048.
- **Horizon**: One forward pass. The future is appended as masked (NaN) patches, and the recurrent state carries them. Maximum 320 steps (10 patches, `future_len`). The code warns and truncates longer horizons to 320. There is no rollout beyond that in the open release.
- **Output**: 9 quantiles, 0.1 to 0.9 (config). Trained on 99 levels, 0.01 to 0.99 (paper).
- **API**: `pip install tirex-2` (0.5.0). Tutorial wrapper `TiRex2`: `from tirex2 import load_model, TimeseriesType`, `model = load_model("NX-AI/TiRex-2", device=DEVICE)`, one `TimeseriesType(target=(V, T), past_covariates=(C, T), future_covariates=(F, T + H))` per series, `model.forecast(series_list, prediction_length=H, output_type="numpy")`. Each output is `(V, 9, H)`, which the wrapper transposes to `(n_series, [V,] H, 9)`.
- **Training**: About 30M Chronos series, about 15M synthetic Gaussian-process series, about 2.5M GIFT-Eval pretraining series, dysts chaotic trajectories at 1%. Multivariate samples (1 to 12 variates) built on the fly by a synthetic coupling pipeline (linear mixing, linear and nonlinear SCMs, cointegration, functional coupling). 700k steps on 22 H100, then long-context post-training (paper). HF tags list `Salesforce/lotsa_data` and `autogluon/chronos_datasets`. Separate decontaminated checkpoints exist for fev-bench and GIFT-Eval.
- **Benchmarks reported**: Author-reported. State-of-the-art zero-shot on GIFT-Eval and fev-bench, lowest mean MASE among nine models on fev-bench. Exact numbers were not extracted from the paper (results section not readable through the fetch tool). The t0 report (third party) ranks TiRex-2 fourth on fev-bench, after TimesFM-3.0, Chronos-2 and t0-beta.
- **Paper / links**: arXiv 2607.01204. Model card https://huggingface.co/NX-AI/TiRex-2. Repo https://github.com/NX-AI/tirex-2. Docs https://nx-ai.github.io/tirex-2/.

## Timestral

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: Mistral AI (Paris). Released 15 October 2026 (blog). Weights under a Modified MIT license, code under Apache 2.0. Four sizes: 5M (5.21M, 8 layers, d=192), 28M, 123M, 209M (24 layers, d=768). All share one architecture and recipe.
- **Architecture type**: Causal patch transformer with Decoupled Horizon Forecasting (DHF). The context is encoded causally, and separate learned forecast tokens read it. Not encoder-only, despite the tutorial wrapper docstring. Layers alternate time attention and variate attention (209M: one `time_variate` block every four, config `block_schedule`). Each variate is a target, a past-only covariate or a known-future covariate. Information flows only from covariates to targets. Multivariate targets are forecast jointly.
- **Tokenization / patching**: Patches of 32 steps, embedded with missing-value indicators. Causal per-patch location and scale, then arcsinh (`use_asinh: true`). Forecast patches are denormalized with the context statistics and the block's earlier predicted medians.
- **Context**: Up to 32,768 steps (1,024 patches, config `context_length`). Longer contexts are cut to the most recent 32,768 steps.
- **Horizon**: Direct, up to 1,024 steps (32 patches, `block_size`) per pass. Longer horizons are forecast block by block, with each block's median fed back as context.
- **Output**: 21 quantiles: 0.01, 0.05, 0.1 to 0.9 by 0.05, 0.95, 0.99. Non-crossing by construction. By default the forecaster averages with the mirrored forecast of the negated series (`symmetric_averaging=True`) and clips at zero for non-negative histories (`clip_nonnegative=True`).
- **API**: `pip install git+https://github.com/mistralai/timestral.git` (1.0.0 installed). Tutorial wrapper `Timestral`: `from timestral import TimestralForecaster`, `TimestralForecaster.from_pretrained("mistralai/Timestral-209M", device=DEVICE)`, `forecast(context, prediction_length=H, past_covariates=(B, C, T), known_covariates=(B, F, T + H))`. Context is `(B, V, T)`, `(B, T)` or `(T,)`. `forecast.quantiles` is `(B, V, H, 21)`, and `forecast.quantile(q)` gives one level.
- **Training**: Stage 1 on purely synthetic data (univariate processes combined into multivariate series with nonlinear dependencies), context 8192. Stage 2 post-training on real plus synthetic data with a 4x longer context. Pinball loss over 21 quantiles (blog). Named corpora and dataset sizes are not stated (blog, HF card, README checked). Tech report "coming soon" on HF.
- **Benchmarks reported**: Vendor claims for 209M: first among zero-shot models on GIFT-Eval (MASE 0.658, CRPS 0.446), TIME (MASE 0.626, CRPS 0.522) and fev-bench (SQL skill 50.39, MASE skill 39.49). 5M: GIFT-Eval MASE 0.705 and CRPS 0.479, TIME MASE 0.652 and CRPS 0.545, fev-bench SQL skill 46.19. Vendor latency: 17 ms (5M) and 113 ms (209M) per forecast on an 8-thread CPU.
- **Paper / links**: No paper yet. Blog https://mistral.ai/news/timestral/. Model cards https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-209M and https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-5M. Repo https://github.com/mistralai/timestral.

## t0 (t0-gamma)

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: The Forecasting Company (Paris). t0-alpha (102M) and t0-beta (256M) in arXiv 2609.24559 (21 September 2026). t0-gamma is a later checkpoint, not covered by the paper. Its release date is not stated on the HF card (secondary sources give October 2026). Apache 2.0. About 256M parameters (README table).
- **Architecture type**: Decoder-style patch transformer. 24 layers in a pattern of 2 time-attention layers then 1 variate (group) attention layer, embedding 1024, FFN 2048 SwiGLU, 8 heads, RoPE on the time axis (config, README, paper for alpha). Extra past variates in `context` are forecast jointly with the target. Known-future covariates condition the forecast but are not forecast.
- **Tokenization / patching**: Patches of 32 steps. Causal mean and std scaling, then arcsinh (`scaler_use_arcsinh`, `scaler_eps_mode: std_clamp`).
- **Context**: No hard cap in the tfc-t0 0.6.1 code (checked `forecaster.py` and `rollout.py`). Training windows are 8192 steps, and the paper's benchmark runs trim to the last 8192 steps. Accuracy is within 1% of full context from 4096 steps (paper, t0-alpha).
- **Horizon**: Direct. Up to 1024 steps (32 patches) in one pass, with the horizon appended as blanked patches. Beyond that, an autoregressive rollout by 1024-step chunks that follows one path per native quantile level and reduces them, as in Chronos-2.
- **Output**: 21 native quantile levels, 0.01 to 0.99 (config). Other levels are interpolated, and levels beyond 0.01 and 0.99 are extrapolated with exponential tails. Default request is (0.1, 0.5, 0.9).
- **API**: `pip install "tfc-t0[torch]>=0.6.0"` (0.6.1 installed). Tutorial wrapper `T0`: `from t0 import T0Forecaster`, `T0Forecaster.from_pretrained("theforecastingcompany/t0-gamma").to(DEVICE).eval()`, `predict(context, horizon=H, quantile_levels=QUANTILES, future_covariates=(B, F, T + H), trim_leading_missing=True, clip_nonnegative=True)`. Context is `(B, T)` or `(B, V, T)`. `out.quantiles` is `(B, H, Q)` or `(B, V, H, Q)`. The four recommended adjustments (`trim_leading_missing`, `symmetric_averaging`, `clip_nonnegative`, `snap_integers`) are off by default.
- **Training**: For t0-alpha (paper): GIFT-Eval pretraining split rebalanced (2.97M series, 24.9B steps) plus four synthetic families: TSMixup-style mixing (10M series), kernel composition (10M), causal-graph SCMs with past and future covariates (1M), covariate effects (0.1M). Curriculum raises the multivariate share from 10% to 40%. 8192-step windows, 150k steps. t0-beta corpora exclude every fev-bench dataset. t0-gamma training data: not stated (HF card and paper checked).
- **Benchmarks reported**: Author-reported on the t0-gamma card: GIFT-Eval CRPS 0.4503 and MASE 0.6580, fev-bench skill 49.40, TIME CRPS 0.5340 and MASE 0.6370. The Timestral card lists t0-gamma at GIFT-Eval CRPS 0.452 and MASE 0.661, a small mismatch. Paper (t0-beta): third on GIFT-Eval (CRPS 0.4738, MASE 0.6865) and fev-bench (skill 46.7).
- **Paper / links**: arXiv 2609.24559. Model card https://huggingface.co/theforecastingcompany/t0-gamma. Repo https://github.com/theforecastingcompany/tfc-t0.

## TabICL forecasting

- **Organisation, release, license, size**: Inria (Soda team). TabICLv2 regressor checkpoint `tabicl-regressor-v2-20260212.ckpt`. TabICLv2 paper arXiv 2602.11139, ICML 2026. BSD 3-Clause. The `tabicl/forecast` code is derived from TabPFN-TS (Prior Labs) under Apache 2.0. About 28M parameters for the TabICLv2 architecture (paper). No separate count is given for the regressor.
- **Architecture type**: Tabular in-context learner, not a time series model. `TabICLForecaster` fits one `TabICLRegressor` per series, with past timesteps as training rows and horizon timesteps as test rows. Series are not mixed. Covariates are extra feature columns of the same table.
- **Tokenization / patching**: No patches. Rows are timesteps. Default features (`temporal_features=["index", "datetime", "periodic"]`): a running index, calendar sin/cos pairs (second, minute, hour, day of week, day of month, day of year, week of year, month) plus raw year, and sin/cos of up to 5 seasonal periods detected by FFT on the context. Known covariates are added as columns. Scaling and outlier clipping are done inside `TabICLRegressor`.
- **Context**: `max_context_length` 4096 by default (the tutorial uses 2048). Only the last rows are kept. Missing targets in the context are dropped.
- **Horizon**: Direct. All horizon rows are predicted in one pass from their features. No lags and not autoregressive, so there is no maximum horizon.
- **Output**: Quantiles at any requested levels, by default 0.1 to 0.9, plus a point estimate (mean by default). The v2 regressor predicts 999 quantiles internally (paper).
- **API**: `pip install "tabicl[forecast]"` (2.2.0). Tutorial wrapper `TabICL`: `from tabicl.forecast import TabICLForecaster`, `TabICLForecaster(max_context_length=2048)`, `predict_df(context_df, future_df=future_df, quantiles=QUANTILES)`. `context_df` has `item_id`, `timestamp`, `target` and covariate columns. `future_df` has the same columns without `target`. The output DataFrame has one row per (item, horizon step) and one column per quantile, reshaped to `(n_series, H, 9)`.
- **Training**: No time series pretraining. TabICLv2 is pretrained on about 35M synthetic tables from a prior of random DAGs with 8 function types (MLP, tree ensemble, GP, linear, quadratic, product and others). Up to 100 features, 1,024 to 60K rows over three stages (500k, 40k and 10k steps) (paper).
- **Benchmarks reported**: Tabular only, author-reported. TabArena: TabICLv2 without tuning beats a tuned, ensembled RealTabPFN-2.5. TALENT: win rate above 50% against RealTabPFN-2.5. Forecasting: not evaluated. The docs state that the forecaster "has not yet been evaluated on a large public benchmark".
- **Paper / links**: arXiv 2602.11139 (TabICLv2) and 2502.05564 (TabICL). Checkpoint https://huggingface.co/jingang/TabICL. Repo https://github.com/soda-inria/tabicl. Forecasting docs https://tabicl.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorials/time_series_forecasting.html.
